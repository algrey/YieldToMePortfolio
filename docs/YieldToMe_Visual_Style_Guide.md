# YieldToMe — Visual Style Guide

## Brand Character

Modern, intelligent and restrained financial design. The brand should feel credible enough for serious investors while remaining approachable and contemporary. The visual language sits between a premium investment research product and a clean consumer finance app.

Core qualities:

- Calm, analytical and confident
- Modern rather than corporate
- Premium without appearing ornate
- Data-led, but not visually cluttered
- Long-term investing rather than speculative trading

---

## Primary Brand Mark

Use a four-bar vertical chart symbol.

### Bar Pattern

The four bars should follow this sequence:

1. Short
2. Taller
3. Slightly shorter
4. Tallest

This creates a recognisable **up–down–up** rhythm rather than a simple staircase.

### Construction

- Four solid vertical rectangles
- Equal bar widths
- Consistent spacing
- Flat lower baseline
- Slightly softened edges are acceptable, but avoid rounded pill shapes
- Use as a standalone app icon, favicon, loading mark or beside the wordmark

### Meaning

The mark represents:

- Market cycles rather than uninterrupted growth
- Progress through volatility
- Portfolio performance
- Compounding over time

---

## Wordmark

**YieldToMe**

Use an elegant, high-contrast editorial serif with a modern financial feel.

### Font Direction

Suitable references:

- Canela
- Editorial New
- Cormorant Garamond
- DM Serif Display
- Playfair Display
- Instrument Serif

Preferred treatment:

- Uppercase **Y**, **T** and **M**
- Tight but comfortable kerning
- High contrast between thick and thin strokes
- White, warm white or pale cream on dark backgrounds
- Avoid heavy, traditional banking-style serif fonts

The wordmark should feel editorial and refined, while the surrounding interface uses a clean sans-serif.

---

## Supporting Typography

Use a neutral modern sans-serif for navigation, labels and body copy.

Suitable references:

- Inter
- Geist
- SF Pro
- Manrope
- Helvetica Now
- Neue Montreal

### Hierarchy

- Wordmark: high-contrast serif
- Major headings: serif or large clean sans-serif
- Interface labels: uppercase sans-serif with generous tracking
- Body copy: regular sans-serif
- Data values: tabular sans-serif numerals

---

## Colour Palette

**Near-black green**

- HEX: `#06110F`
- Use for hero panels, app backgrounds and large brand surfaces

### Secondary Background

**Deep forest**

- HEX: `#0B1C18`
- Use for cards, subtle gradients and panel separation

### Primary Green

**Fresh investment green**

- HEX: `#63C878`
- Use for the bar mark, positive data, links and active states

### Bright Accent Green

- HEX: `#84E88D`
- Use sparingly for highlights, chart endpoints and glow effects

### Warm White

- HEX: `#F2EFE7`
- Use for the wordmark and primary headings

### Muted Text

- HEX: `#A5ADA8`
- Use for descriptions, metadata and secondary labels

### Divider

- HEX: `#22322D`
- Use for thin borders and separators

## Layout Style

- Large areas of negative space
- Dark edge-to-edge backgrounds
- Fine dividers rather than heavy card borders
- Strong alignment to a grid
- Minimal decoration
- One dominant brand element per composition
- No simulated app screenshots unless the image is specifically intended to represent the real product
- Prefer abstract data, typography and brand geometry over fake interface content

---

## Chart and Data Style

- Thin green line charts
- Very subtle grid lines
- Low-opacity green area fills
- Small, precise chart markers
- Avoid highly jagged speculative-trading imagery
- Show believable movement with periods of decline and recovery
- Use glow only at one focal point, not across the full chart

The four-bar brand mark should remain separate from decorative charts and must preserve its exact up–down–up silhouette.

---

## Iconography

- Thin-line icons
- Consistent stroke weight
- Geometric and minimal
- Green outlines on dark backgrounds
- Avoid skeuomorphic coins, dollar signs, bulls, bears and candlestick clichés

Preferred themes:

- Portfolio allocation
- Macro and global markets
- News and research
- Value and long-term conviction

---

## Photography and Illustration

Preferred visual treatments:

- Abstract market geometry
- Subtle topographic or contour patterns
- Dark architectural forms
- Soft gradients
- Clean editorial compositions
- Sparse global or macro references

Avoid:

- Traders shouting on exchange floors
- Stacks of coins
- Generic business handshakes
- Luxury watches and cars
- Neon crypto aesthetics
- Excessive gold
- Cartoon finance imagery

---

## Brand Copy Style

Short, controlled and confident.

Examples:

**Markets. Macro. Value.**

**Portfolio · Macro · News · Insights**

**Better decisions. Stronger returns.**

**See the big picture.**

**Understand what moves markets.**

**Find value. Think long term.**

Avoid exaggerated promises, aggressive trading language and claims of guaranteed performance.

# Master Image Prompt

Create a premium modern brand image for **YieldToMe**, a portfolio tracking, macroeconomic news and value-investing platform.

Use a deep near-black green background with subtle forest-green gradients. Place a distinctive four-bar chart symbol prominently near the YieldToMe wordmark. The bars must follow an exact **short, taller, slightly shorter, tallest** sequence, creating an up–down–up rhythm. Use solid rectangular bars with equal widths and spacing.

Set **YieldToMe** in a refined high-contrast editorial serif typeface, with elegant thin and thick strokes, similar in character to Canela, Editorial New or Instrument Serif. Use warm white for the wordmark and fresh muted green for the bar symbol and supporting text.

The composition should feel like a modern premium consumer finance brand: clean, restrained, intelligent and data-led, with the visual simplicity of a leading investment app. Use generous negative space, precise alignment, thin dividers and subtle data-inspired geometry. Add a restrained green market line or abstract chart pattern only as a secondary visual element. The line should show realistic cycles, including rises, dips and recovery, rather than uninterrupted growth.

Do not show a phone, computer, dashboard mock-up or fictional app interface. Do not use coins, dollar signs, candlestick clichés, gold luxury imagery or generic stock photography.

Include the supporting line:

**PORTFOLIO · MACRO · NEWS · INSIGHTS**

Overall mood: dark, modern, editorial, calm, credible and sophisticated.
